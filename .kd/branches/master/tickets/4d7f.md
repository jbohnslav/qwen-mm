# Select and integrate the fastest suitable Rust image resizer

D4 diagnostics show the scalar source-faithful Pillow resize port makes
single-image preprocessing roughly 45-49% slower than the official Pillow
baseline. Replace it with the fastest maintained open-source Rust resizer that
preserves structural processor correctness and beats the official end-to-end
baseline in every applicable image case. Pillow pixel identity is no longer a
selection goal.

## Scope

- Benchmark the official Pillow 12.3.0 resize boundary separately from Python,
  decode, normalize, and patchify overhead.
- Evaluate maintained open-source Rust-native candidates, including at least
  `fast_image_resize` and `pic-scale`, with `image`/`resize` retained as useful
  references. Prefer runtime-dispatched SIMD and caller-owned RGB8 buffers
  without adding a heavyweight system dependency.
- Compare relevant filters as performance/quality choices; do not require the
  winner to reproduce Pillow's bicubic weights, rounding, or adversarial pixels.
- Freeze a candidate-independent neural-image fidelity envelope before selecting
  the winner. Preserve exact geometry, keys, dtypes, shapes, strides, grids,
  ordering, limits, and stable errors; report resize/tensor RMSE, p50/p90/p99,
  maximum error, bias, and representative natural-image diagnostics.
- Record dependency, wheel/distribution, platform, memory, and thread-budget
  tradeoffs as well as latency.

## Non-goals

Do not add an OpenCV/libvips C/C++ wrapper or another heavyweight runtime merely
to obtain a resizer. Do not spend this ticket hand-writing another complete
resize implementation before measuring maintained Rust libraries. Do not trade
away structural correctness, robust input/error behavior, or obvious natural-
image fidelity. Adversarial pixel identity to Pillow is explicitly not a gate.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The report distinguishes the current still-image path—Pillow bicubic in
      `qwen_vl_utils`, followed by Transformers with `do_resize=False`—from the
      separate TorchVision video path.
- [ ] At least two maintained optimized Rust implementations and the current
      scalar port are measured under identical inputs, output dimensions, warm
      state, and thread budgets on ARM and x86 where available.
- [ ] A candidate-independent natural-image numerical fidelity envelope is
      documented and frozen before performance selection; the winner passes it
      while all structural processor outputs and errors remain exact.
- [ ] The selected option beats the official end-to-end baseline in every
      applicable D4 image case and both comparable thread regimes, profiles, and
      controlled architectures; the existing 2x headline batch gates remain.
- [ ] The winner does not regress D4 memory, parallel-efficiency, packaging, or
      one-total-thread-budget requirements.
- [ ] The revised compatibility/conformance corpus, full repository checks, and
      controlled D4 certification pass for the implementation change.

## Worklog

### 2026-08-13 — ticket created from D4 resize review

The current still-image implementation is a hand-written, scalar,
source-faithful port of Pillow 12.3.0's separable fixed-point resampler. The
top-level resize-stage contract permits a per-channel byte difference of one;
the selected port happens to be exact on the 17-case frozen corpus, but
universal bit-exactness was not the stated requirement. The only optimized Rust
candidate previously recorded was `fast_image_resize` 6.1.0 Catmull-Rom. It was
non-identical in 12 of 17 image cases, exceeded the then-stated one-byte bound
in 6 of 17, and differed by up to 21 byte levels. OpenCV and other mature native
backends were not evaluated. Related active certification ticket: `050f`.

### 2026-08-13 — King prioritizes neural preprocessing speed over Pillow identity

The King explicitly authorized relaxing image-resize numeric exactness. The
product requirement is now to select the fastest suitable open-source Rust
resizer and beat the official baseline in every applicable image benchmark
case. Exact geometry, public structure, metadata, types, ordering, resource
limits, and error semantics remain required; adversarial per-pixel equality to
Pillow does not. Heavy OpenCV-style wrappers and another speculative hand-rolled
kernel are disfavored. D4 and the Phase D epic now depend on this ticket.
