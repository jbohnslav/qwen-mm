## Goal

Determine and remove the controlled-host timing instability that placed the
x86 Qwen3.5 official `rgb24/t1` process medians just above the frozen noise
limit, then obtain a valid retained-sample comparison.

## Scope

- Inspect affinity, host isolation, frequency/runtime state, process ordering,
  warmup behavior, and per-repetition official timings on the controlled x86

gate, or hide official variance by aggregating supplemental timing-floor work
into the latency distribution.
