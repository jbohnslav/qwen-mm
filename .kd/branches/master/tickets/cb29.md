# D4a: Wire the compact performance capture pipeline

## Goal

Finish the compact D4 benchmark as a complete, validated capture path. Reuse
the smoke entry point and compact planner already implemented at `bb50c65`, and
make both the local ARM and controlled Linux/x86 launchers execute the selected
84-subprocess shipping-build matrix instead of the legacy 1,360-subprocess
protocol.

## Scope

- Route the local ARM and controlled Linux/Modal entry points through the
  compact capture planner and executor already present in the benchmark code.
- Keep both profiles and these seven case/thread coordinates: `image24` at
  t1/t8, `ragged24` at t8, `image1` at t1, `rgb24` at t1, `images_16` at t8,
  and `text_long` at t1.
- Use the shipping build, three fresh process repetitions per implementation,
  randomized AB/BA ordering, at least 30 measured samples, and a five-second
  timing floor for every selected coordinate.
- Update the archive schema, inventory/currentness checks, evaluator, and report
  inputs for the compact matrix while retaining exact source, artifact, host,
  runtime, affinity, and thread-budget provenance.
- Continue through all measured gates and return a complete list of misses;
  fail closed on infrastructure, host-control, conformance, provenance, or
  evidence-integrity failures that invalidate the capture.
- Reduce the controlled x86 request to the resources actually needed by the
  eight-thread matrix and print a small hard time/cost ceiling before any
  remote worker can be created.

## Non-goals

Do not launch paid compute, rerun the legacy exhaustive matrix, change roadmap
thresholds, reimplement the completed local smoke test, or publish a final
cross-architecture certification result. This ticket makes the experiment
runnable and reviewable; `cd33` owns controlled execution and publication.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] `make d4-local` and the controlled Linux/x86 entry point execute the
      compact shipping-only 84-subprocess plan, and a dry-run exposes the exact
      selected coordinates, repetitions, sample floor, and timing floor.
- [x] The compact archive schema and currentness/inventory validation reject
      missing, duplicated, stale, or out-of-plan observations and authenticate
      the required source/build/host/runtime/thread provenance.
- [x] The evaluator accepts separate valid compact ARM and x86 archives,
      evaluates every selected speed, regression, scaling, and memory gate, and
      reports all measured misses together after post-capture conformance.
- [x] Infrastructure, control, conformance, provenance, and evidence-integrity
      failures still abort without being mislabeled as performance misses.
- [x] The x86 launcher requests no more than 8 CPUs and 16 GiB unless new
      evidence justifies otherwise, prints an explicit hard time/cost ceiling,
      and cannot create paid compute without a separate approval step.
- [x] Focused D4 tests, dry runs, schema/report validation fixtures, and the
      repository correctness checks pass; the existing `make d4-smoke` remains
      a roughly one-to-two-minute no-uv gut check.
