# Post-v0.1 certification: reduce ARM batch transient peaks

## Goal

Reduce ARM batch scratch and allocation lifetimes so the failing `images_16`
and `ragged24` coordinates satisfy the frozen per-process transient-memory
ratio without reintroducing a full float-CHW intermediate.

## Scope

- Profile native peak categories, external RSS deltas, scratch arenas, resize
  buffers, and caller-owned output lifetimes for the three failed ARM batch
  coordinates.
- Reuse or shorten allocations only where lifetime evidence proves safety.
- Preserve two-phase exact-capacity behavior, deterministic ordering, dtypes,
  errors, and the neural-image fidelity contract.

## Non-goals

Do not alter the `<=0.5` ratio, discard repetitions, substitute a different
denominator, or treat a near-zero official RSS delta as permission to suppress
candidate-native peak accounting.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `memory/arm64/qwen3-vl-8b/images_16/t8`,
      `memory/arm64/qwen3-vl-8b/ragged24/t8`, and
      `memory/arm64/qwen3.5-9b/images_16/t8` each have positive official
      denominators and every paired candidate ratio `<=0.5`.
- [ ] Allocation/lifetime evidence explains the prior native peaks and proves
      which buffers or live ranges were removed, reused, or shortened.
- [ ] No full float-CHW batch intermediate, unbounded cache, nested pool, or
      output-contract change is introduced.
- [ ] Full image conformance, repository correctness, and D4 evidence
      validation pass with exact-source provenance recorded.
