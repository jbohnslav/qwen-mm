# Recover ARM raw-RGB speed and transient-memory margin

## Goal

Recover the ARM `rgb24/t1` raw-frame path so both shipping profiles are
statistically faster than the official processor and stay within the frozen
candidate-to-official transient-memory ratio.

## Scope

- Profile raw-RGB planning, conversion, patchification, allocation, and copy
  lifetimes on the controlled ARM host for both profiles.
- Remove or reuse only buffers/copies justified by the profile, preserving the
  exact public structure, errors, dtypes, ordering, and pixel contract.
- Re-run the full image conformance suite and the frozen compact coordinates
  needed to prove both speed and memory recovery.

## Non-goals

Do not change the `>1.0x` paired-bootstrap speed lower bound, the `<=0.5`
transient-memory ratio, benchmark sample selection, or official baseline. Do
not expand this ticket to encoded video or the vLLM integration.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `speed/arm64/qwen3-vl-8b/rgb24/t1` and
      `speed/arm64/qwen3.5-9b/rgb24/t1` both have paired-bootstrap lower bounds
      strictly above `1.0x` under the frozen compact protocol.
- [ ] `memory/arm64/qwen3-vl-8b/rgb24/t1` and
      `memory/arm64/qwen3.5-9b/rgb24/t1` both have every positive official
      denominator and every paired candidate ratio `<=0.5`.
- [ ] Profile evidence identifies the dominant raw-RGB costs, and each accepted
      optimization is measured in isolation without nested-pool
      oversubscription.
- [ ] Full image conformance, repository correctness, and D4 evidence
      validation pass with commands and exact-source provenance recorded.
